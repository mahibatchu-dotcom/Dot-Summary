# Line 1:  Closing The Loop: IIT Bombay Controversy and Tata Sons Leadership Update
*2026-09-26* · [Original article](https://www.dot.news/post/6ab6b762e3f51700020ba255)

**In one line:** IIT Bombay faces scrutiny and protests following a student's suicide and allegations of caste discrimination, while Tata Sons announces that Chairman N. Chandrasekaran will stay on for another term.

**Key points:**
- Sahil Wakode, a second-year IIT Bombay student, died by suicide after being caught using a mobile phone during an exam.
- Sahil's family filed an FIR under the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act, alleging he faced caste-based abuse from Professor Suryanarayana Doolla on the director's instructions.
- Following student protests and an 18-point charter agreement, Professor Doolla was suspended from his administrative role pending an investigation.
- This marks the third suicide at IIT Bombay in a six-month period, sparking discussions on student mental health and campus environment.
- In a leadership update, Tata Sons reversed a previous announcement and stated that Chairman N. Chandrasekaran has agreed to stay for a new five-year term.

**Numbers and names to remember:**
- Sahil Wakode: IIT Bombay student who died by suicide.
- Professor Suryanarayana Doolla: Invigilator and Dean of Administrative Affairs at IIT Bombay.
- Shri Shireesh B. Kedare: Director of IIT Bombay.
- Ramdas Athawale: Union Minister of State for Social Justice and Empowerment.
- N. Chandrasekaran: Chairman of Tata Sons.

**Why it matters:** The tragic death at IIT Bombay highlights critical issues regarding student mental health, institutional procedures during academic malpractice, and allegations of caste discrimination at elite educational institutions. Meanwhile, the Tata Sons leadership reversal settles uncertainty at the top of one of India's biggest business groups.

**Key terms:**
- Invigilating: Acting as an official supervisor during an examination.
- Scheduled Caste (SC): An officially recognized historically disadvantaged social group in India.
